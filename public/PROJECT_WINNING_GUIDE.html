📌 1. Project Essence: What is BIS Saathi?

BIS Saathi is an authoritative, Government-grade Multimodal Agentic AI Assistant designed for the Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS). It solves the dual regulatory challenge of Indian manufacturing and citizen consumer protection:

  • For Industry / MSMEs: Simplifies 350+ mandatory Quality Control Orders (QCOs), cuts certification lead time by 60%, generates pre-audit document checklists, and tracks live scrutiny timelines.
  • For Everyday Citizens: Instantly detects counterfeit ISI marks using AI camera vision, verifies CM/L & Gold HUID numbers, and enables 1-click evidence-backed grievance redressal.

🏗️ 2. Architectural Blueprint: How We Built It

Why This Exact Stack? (The Engineering Logic)

Every single technology in this project was hand-picked for specific performance, regulatory, and scalability reasons:

Next.js 16 (App Router + Turbopack)

Provides Server-Side Rendering (SSR) for instant first-contentful paint, Edge Route Handlers for high-speed AI streaming, and modular component architecture.

Google Gemini 3.6 Flash

State-of-the-art multimodal reasoning. Processes high-resolution product mark photos, parses dense technical PDF test reports, and generates structured compliance citations.

Supabase pgvector (768-Dim)

PostgreSQL-native vector index using Cosine Similarity. Eliminates separate vector DB overhead by housing application tables and standard chunk embeddings in a single ACID-compliant store.

GIGW 3.0 & IS 17802:2021

Official Indian standard for web accessibility. Incorporates A- A A+ font resizing, High Contrast Mode, Screen Reader ARIA semantics, and authentic State Emblems.

⚡ 3. The 8 Core Feature Engines

1. Multimodal RAG Consultation Cockpit (/chat)

A 3-column workspace with Gemini 3.6 Flash. Grounded strictly on vector-indexed Gazette notifications and Indian Standards. Users can ask questions via text or voice (Speech-to-Text), listen to audio readouts, and inspect uploaded PDF test reports.

2. ChatGPT / Gemini Persistent Session Manager

Full multi-session management stored persistently in localStorage. Supports "+ New Consultation", automated session title generation based on the first query, seamless switching, and deletion.

3. AI Photo & Camera Mark Scanner (/checker)

Consumers can snap a picture of any product packaging. Gemini AI Vision analyzes standard geometry, typography, and validates the 7-digit CM/L-XXXXXXX or R-XXXXXXXX license code against registered formats.

4. 1-Click Agentic "Export Compliance Dossier"

On any technical consultation response, clicking "Export Dossier" synthesizes the dialogue into a formal, downloadable Markdown compliance audit report ready for factory submission.

5. Certification Pathways & Document Checklist Generator (/pathway)

Interactive comparison of Scheme-I (Domestic ISI), Scheme-II (CRS), and FMCS (Foreign). Generates official lists of statutory documents (factory lease, SIT in-house lab machinery, calibration certs) and includes a mock application tracking pipeline (APP-2026-XXXX).

6. Grievance Portal with Evidence Upload & Docket Tracker (/complaint)

Formal filing of substandard products under Section 29 of the BIS Act 2016. Drag-and-drop evidence uploads, statutory penalty notices, and docket tracking across CMED enforcement stages.

7. 350+ Mandatory Standards & QCO Directory (/standards)

Real-time searchable catalog of mandatory standards (Helmets, Electric Kettles, Toys, Pressure Cookers, LED lights, Gold Hallmarking) showing audit requirements, testing lead times, and direct AI consultation links.

8. Genuine vs Counterfeit Visual Inspection Guide (/checker)

Side-by-side comparative inspection breakdown for citizens: Domestic ISI (with vs without CM/L), CRS (registered vs fake), and Gold Hallmarking (3-symbol HUID vs old 4-mark fake).

Q2 "Why did you choose Supabase pgvector instead of a standalone vector database like Pinecone or ChromaDB?"

Your Answer: "Sir, in a production government application, data consistency is critical. By using PostgreSQL with the pgvector extension, we achieve a Unified Relational + Vector Architecture. We can perform vector similarity searches and relational joins with complaints, license records, and user sessions in a single ACID-compliant database, eliminating the network latency and data synchronization overhead of multi-database architectures."

💡 Why this impresses: Shows architectural maturity and understanding of distributed systems trade-offs.
Q3 "How does the AI verify fake marks from an uploaded image?"

💡 Why this impresses: Proves that the vision pipeline is doing structural inspection, not just basic text OCR.
