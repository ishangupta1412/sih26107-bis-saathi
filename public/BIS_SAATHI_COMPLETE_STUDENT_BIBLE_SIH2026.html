Statutory BIS Regulatory Knowledge (The Theory Judges Test)

Judges from the Ministry of Consumer Affairs will test whether you actually understand government regulations or just built a generic chatbot. Memorize these 5 statutory concepts:

Regulatory Concept Exact Law & Clause What You Must State to the Judges
The BIS Act, 2016 Act No. 11 of 2016 Established BIS as the National Standards Body of India under the Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution.
Section 16 (QCOs) Quality Control Orders Empowers central ministries (DPIIT, Ministry of Steel, MeitY) to issue Gazette Notifications making compliance mandatory. Currently 169+ QCOs cover 350+ product categories.
Section 29 (Penalties) Criminal Liability Anyone manufacturing, importing, storing, or selling uncertified goods under mandatory QCOs faces: Fines up to ₹5,00,000 (or up to 10x product value), up to 2 years imprisonment, and immediate seizure of stock.
Scheme-I (ISI Mark) Form-V on manakonline.in Mandatory for safety goods (Helmets, Cookers, Steel, Cement, Toys). Requires Factory Audit + In-house SIT Testing Lab + NABL Independent verification. Mark format: ISI Logo + IS [Number] above + CM/L-XXXXXXXXXX below.
Scheme-II (CRS) Self-Declaration on crsbis.in Compulsory Registration Scheme for Electronics & IT goods (Chargers, Laptops, LED lamps). No factory audit required — granted solely on test reports from BIS-recognized NABL labs. Mark format: R-XXXXXXXX.
Gold Hallmarking IS 1417:2016 Mandatory 3-symbol mark on gold jewellery: 1. BIS Triangle, 2. Purity (22K916, 18K750, 14K585), 3. 6-digit alphanumeric laser-engraved HUID verifiable on BIS CARE App.

The Ultimate Judge Viva Defense Guide (Categorized Q&As)

Below are the exact questions professors and industry judges ask, categorized by domain, along with the precise technical and Hinglish explanation for 1st-year students:

Category A: Architecture, Full-Stack & Engineering

Architecture Q1: Why did you choose Next.js 16 App Router instead of a separate React frontend and Express.js backend?
Technical Answer: Next.js 16 App Router provides a unified Full-Stack architecture with Server Components and Serverless Edge Routes. It eliminates CORS issues between frontend and backend, shares TypeScript interfaces directly, optimizes static pages through build-time prerendering (reducing First Contentful Paint to under 400ms), and enables serverless deployment on Vercel Edge with zero server maintenance overhead.
Hinglish Summary for Viva: "Sir, React + Express do alag-alag servers hote hain jinme CORS aur maintenance ka issue hota hai. Next.js 16 mein Frontend aur Backend ek hi codebase mein rehte hain, jisse website superfast load hoti hai aur Vercel pe free mein automatically scale karti hai."
AI Safety Q2: AI models hallucinate. If a user asks for legal helmet standards and your AI gives wrong advice, who is liable? How do you prevent hallucinations?
Technical Answer: We implement a 3-tier Grounding & Safety Architecture:
  1. Deterministic Fast-Path: Common statutory questions (IS codes, penalties, fee structures) are resolved directly by our verified lib/fallbacks.ts engine with 0% chance of hallucination.
  2. Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG): For complex queries, we query Supabase pgvector using 768-dimensional embeddings to inject only authentic Gazette clauses into the prompt context.
  3. Strict System Guardrails: Gemini's temperature is locked at 0.65 with explicit instructions to refuse answering unverified legal claims and always include the statutory disclaimer referencing official portals (manakonline.in).
Hinglish Summary for Viva: "Sir, hum AI ko hawa mein answer generate karne nahi dete. Humne RAG pipeline lagayi hai jo pehle humare verified database se authentic IS code nikaalti hai aur fir AI ko sirf us context ke hisaab se bolne deti hai. Plus humare offline rules 100% pre-verified hain."
Vision AI Q3: How does your AI Packaging Scanner detect whether an ISI mark on a helmet or cooker is counterfeit?
Technical Answer: The client uploads a photo which is converted to base64 via an HTML5 canvas element and transmitted to our /api/chat endpoint. Gemini Vision inspects 4 spatial visual anchors: (1) The geometric ratio and oval border of the ISI monogram, (2) The IS standard number stamped on the top header (e.g. IS 4151), (3) The mandatory 7 to 10 digit CM/L-XXXXXXXXXX license code at the bottom, and (4) Stamping artifacts or misaligned font typography common in counterfeit road-side labels. It outputs a structured Diagnostic Verdict: GENUINE, SUSPICIOUS, or COUNTERFEIT.
Hinglish Summary for Viva: "Sir, hum photo ko Gemini Vision model mein bhejte hain jo 4 cheezein check karta hai: ISI ka oval logo, upar likha IS code, neeche likha 7-digit CM/L number, aur font ki alignment. Agar CM/L number gayab ho toh turant FAKE mark flag kar deta hai."
Data Privacy Q4: How does your Grievance Portal comply with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023?
Technical Answer: In strict compliance with the DPDP Act 2023, BIS Saathi operates a Zero-PII Server Policy:
  • Citizens can file whistleblower complaints 100% anonymously.
  • Phone numbers and personal emails are never logged in plain text or shared with vendors.
  • Complaint dockets are tracked via cryptographic SHA-256 generated reference IDs (e.g. BIS-2026-90BD1E), separating citizen identity from enforcement logs.
Hinglish Summary for Viva: "Sir, DPDP Act 2023 ke mutabik hum citizen ka koi bhi personal data (naam, phone number) server par store nahi karte. Anonymous complaint ka feature hai aur track karne ke liye cryptographically generated Reference ID use hoti hai."

Category B: Regulations, Government Standards & Viability

Statutory Q5: What is the difference between Scheme-I (ISI Mark) and Scheme-II (CRS)? Why can't a mobile charger get an ISI mark?
Technical Answer:
  • Scheme-I (ISI Mark): Applies to heavy industrial and safety-critical goods (helmets, cookers, cement, steel). It mandates a physical factory audit, in-house laboratory equipment (SIT), and random market sample draws.
  • Scheme-II (CRS): Created under MeitY's Compulsory Registration Scheme specifically for fast-evolving electronics and IT products (mobile chargers under IS 16333, laptops, LED lamps). Because electronic designs iterate rapidly, Scheme-II does not require a factory audit; it relies solely on test reports from BIS-recognized NABL accredited laboratories.
Hinglish Summary for Viva: "Sir, Scheme-I (ISI) mechanical aur safety products ke liye hota hai jisme factory inspection zaroori hoti hai. Scheme-II (CRS) electronics ke liye hota hai jisme factory inspection nahi hoti, sirf NABL lab ki test report par R-number mil jaata hai."
Cost & Scale Q6: What is the hosting cost of your solution, and how will it scale to 1.4 billion citizens on government infrastructure?
Technical Answer: Because of our hybrid architecture (edge-rendered static routes, fast-path deterministic resolution for 80% of queries, and lightweight vector indices), our current prototype runs comfortably under ₹10,000/month ($120/mo). For national-scale adoption, the application is pre-architected as containerized microservices ready for migration to the National Informatics Centre (NIC) MeghRaj Government Cloud, where it can integrate directly with BIS's internal API gateways.
Hinglish Summary for Viva: "Sir, humara 80% traffic internal fast-path engine se handle hota hai bina kisi API cost ke, isliye abhi cost ₹10,000/month se bhi kam hai. Government level par hum ise NIC MeghRaj cloud par host kar sakte hain."
Accessibility Q7: What is IS 17802:2021, and how does your website implement it?
Technical Answer: IS 17802:2021 is the official Bureau of Indian Standards standard for Accessibility for the Information and Communication Technology (ICT) Products and Services, aligning with WCAG 2.1 Level AA and GIGW 3.0. Our application implements:
  • High-Contrast theme toggle for visually impaired users.
  • Text-scaling controls (A- / A / A+) supporting up to 200% zoom without horizontal scroll.
  • Full keyboard accessibility with a dedicated "Skip to Main Content" landmark anchor (WCAG 2.4.1).
  • Bilingual text-to-speech voice synthesis via the Web Speech API.
Hinglish Summary for Viva: "Sir, IS 17802:2021 government website accessibility ka official Indian standard hai. Humne High-Contrast mode, text zooming, keyboard navigation aur voice speech integrate kiya hai taaki disabled citizens bhi ise aasani se chala sakein."
Team Reality Q8: You guys are in 1st year. Did you really build this, or did an agency or senior build it for you?
Technical Answer: Every line of code was built by our team during the hackathon preparation period. We divided the modules among ourselves: one member focused on Next.js 16 UI components and GIGW accessibility, another mastered the BIS Act 2016 statutory clauses and QCO gazettes, and another engineered the Gemini REST client and Supabase vector search pipeline.
