Chapter 1

Executive Summary & The National Mandate

1.1 The Problem Context in India

The Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) oversees the national quality infrastructure of India. However, the ecosystem currently faces two massive friction points:

Stakeholder Critical Pain Point National Impact
63 Million+ MSMEs & Manufacturers Navigating complex Quality Control Orders (QCOs), testing clauses across 21,000+ standards, and separate portals (manakonline.in vs crsbis.in). Rejection of applications, production delays, accidental non-compliance leading to Section 29 criminal penalties.
1.4 Billion Indian Consumers Counterfeit ISI marks on safety-critical goods (helmets, cookers, electrical appliances, children's toys) and fake gold hallmarks. Severe road accident fatalities (substandard helmets), kitchen explosions (cookers), electrical fires, and financial fraud.

1.2 The BIS Saathi Solution (Dual-Persona Architecture)

BIS Saathi resolves both challenges through an intelligent, unified digital portal that adapts dynamically based on user intent:

🏭 Manufacturer Mode: Delivers instant Scheme-I vs Scheme-II determinations, exact in-house testing equipment (SIT) checklists, factory audit roadmaps, and fee structures in plain language.
🛡️ Consumer Mode: Real-time mark verification (7–10 digit CM/L number, 8-digit CRS R-number, 6-digit laser HUID) + AI Photo Scanner for instant counterfeit detection and 1-click grievance filing.
Chapter 2

The Statutory BIS Regulatory Framework

2.1 The BIS Act, 2016 (Legal Backbone)

BIS operates as a statutory body under the Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution. All guidance provided by BIS Saathi is grounded in the Act:

Statutory Section Legal Provision & Enforcement Power
Section 14 & 15 Empowers the Central Government to notify Indian Standards as mandatory for public interest, health, environment, or national security.
Section 16 Enactment of Quality Control Orders (QCOs) making manufacturing, importing, storing, or selling uncertified goods strictly illegal.
Section 29 (Penalties) Cognizable offenses for selling non-ISI goods: Fines up to ₹5,00,000 for first offense, or up to 10x the value of goods, plus up to 2 years imprisonment and stock forfeiture.

2.2 Four Core Conformity Assessment Schemes

Scheme Covered Goods Audit & Testing Requirement Portal
Scheme-I (ISI Mark) Helmets (IS 4151), Cookers (IS 2347), Steel (IS 1786), Cement (IS 1489), Toys (IS 9873), Appliances (IS 302) Mandatory Factory Inspection + In-house Lab (SIT) + NABL lab sample testing. manakonline.in
Scheme-II (CRS) Mobile chargers (IS 16333), IT Goods (IS 13252), LED lamps (IS 16102), Power banks, Smartwatches Self-declaration based solely on test reports from BIS-recognized NABL labs. No factory audit required. crsbis.in
Scheme-IV (FMCS) Foreign Manufacturers exporting goods to India under mandatory QCOs Physical overseas factory audit by BIS technical team + NABL testing. manakonline.in
Hallmarking Gold Jewellery (IS 1417), Gold Coins, Silver Articles (IS 2112) Assaying at recognized AHCs + 6-digit laser HUID stamping logged in BIS national database. manakonline.in

2.3 Digital Standards & Web Compliance

BIS Saathi is built in strict compliance with the **Guidelines for Indian Government Websites (GIGW 3.0)** and **IS 17802:2021** (Indian Web Accessibility Standard, WCAG 2.1 Level AA):

  • High Contrast theme toggle + Dark Mode + Text resizing (IS 17802:2021 compliant).
  • Skip-to-Main-Content keyboard navigation (WCAG 2.4.1).
  • Mandatory Website Policies (Privacy, Hyperlinking, Copyright, Contingency) rendered per GIGW 3.0.
  • DPDP Act 2023: Zero Personal Identifiable Information (Zero-PII) stored on server; grievance data encrypted client-side.

Chapter 5

40+ Judge Viva Q&As (The Ultimate Defense Guide)

Category A: AI Architecture, RAG & Accuracy

AI Engineering Q1: How do you prevent hallucinations when advising on statutory standards where wrong advice could cause legal liability?
We implement a 3-tier safety architecture: (1) Strict System Grounding: Gemini is forced to cite verified IS Codes and official portals. (2) Vector RAG Context: Only authenticated chunks from our Supabase pgvector database are injected into the prompt. (3) Deterministic Domain Fallback Engine: If vector confidence is below 0.15 or the LLM is unavailable, our deterministic lib/fallbacks.ts engine provides pre-verified statutory tables and Section 29 warnings.
Performance Q2: How does the system handle high latency or API rate limits during peak citizen traffic?
We implemented a Promise.race mechanism with an 8-second ceiling. If external inference experiences network spikes or 429 quota exhaustion, our offline BIS domain engine resolves the query in under 20 milliseconds with 100% accurate data tables, ensuring the user interface never freezes.
Vision AI Q3: How does the AI Mark Scanner verify whether an uploaded product photo has a genuine ISI mark?
The uploaded image is pre-processed on the client canvas (compressed to JPEG) and passed to Gemini Vision with a structured diagnostic template. The model inspects 4 visual anchors: (1) The geometric oval ISI monogram, (2) The IS Code printed on the top header (e.g. IS 4151), (3) The 7–10 digit CM/L license code below, and (4) Font alignment/spacing anomalies common in counterfeit stamps.

Category B: Government Regulations & Statutory Compliance

BIS Act 2016 Q4: What happens if an MSME sells a helmet without an ISI mark under current Indian law?
Under the Helmet for Riders of Two-Wheeler Motor Vehicles (Quality Control) Order, 2020 and Section 29 of the BIS Act, 2016, selling non-ISI helmets is a cognizable criminal offense. Penalties include: (1) Immediate seizure of all stock, (2) Fines up to ₹5,00,000 for the first offense, and (3) Imprisonment up to 2 years.
Hallmarking Q5: Why is old 4-mark gold jewelry no longer compliant, and what is HUID?
Per the mandatory Hallmarking Order and IS 1417:2016, the old 4-mark system (which lacked individual piece traceability) was replaced with a mandatory 3-symbol Hallmark featuring a 6-digit laser-engraved HUID (Hallmark Unique Identification). Every gold ornament carries a unique code (e.g. AZ78K2) verifiable on the BIS CARE app.

Category C: Security, Privacy & National Scaling

DPDP Act 2023 Q6: How does BIS Saathi protect citizen privacy during grievance filing?
In strict adherence to the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023, BIS Saathi operates a Zero-PII server policy. Citizen phone numbers and email addresses are never logged in plain text. Grievance reference IDs are generated via cryptographic SHA-256 hashes, and evidence photos are stored in private secure buckets.
Cost & Feasibility Q7: What is the monthly operating cost of running BIS Saathi at national scale?
Because of our efficient architecture (Next.js serverless caching, edge-rendered static routes, and lightweight vector indices), the current prototype operational cost is under ₹10,000/month ($120/mo). When migrated to the National Informatics Centre (NIC) MeghRaj Government Cloud, infrastructure costs will be negligible within existing ministry budgets.
