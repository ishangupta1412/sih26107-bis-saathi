🏛️ CHAPTER 1: THE ORIGIN, MISSION & PROBLEM STATEMENT (SIH26107)

1.1 The Real-World Context (Why was this built?)

India me Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) Ministry of Consumer Affairs ke under aati hai. BIS ka main kaam hai: 1. Indian Standards (IS) formulate karna (jaise helmets ke liye IS 4151, electrical safety ke liye IS 302, pressure cookers ke liye IS 2347, gold jewellery ke liye IS 1417). 2. Quality Control Orders (QCOs) enforce karna taaki substandard ya nakli products market me na bikein. 3. Manufacturers ko certification licenses (ISI Mark, CRS, FMCS) grant karna.

1.2 The Problem (The Big Pain Points):

- MSMEs / Manufacturers ke liye: India me 350+ mandatory QCOs hain. Ek small manufacturer (e.g. helmet ya electric kettle banane wala) confuse ho jata hai ki kaun sa standard apply hota hai, kaun sa scheme select karna hai (Scheme I vs Scheme II vs FMCS), in-house lab me kaun se testing equipments (SIT) lagane hain, aur factory inspection se pehle kaun-kaun se documents ready karne hain. Isme unke 2 se 3 mahine sirf consultancies aur paperwork me waste ho jaate the. - Consumers / Citizens ke liye: Markets me nakli ISI stickers aur uncertified chargers bikte hain jo blast ho jaate hain ya accident me helmet toot jaate hain. Normal citizen ko pata hi nahi hota ki genuine ISI mark pe 7 se 10 digit ka CM/L-XXXXXXXXXX number hota hai ya gold pe 6-digit laser HUID hota hai. - Language Barrier: Government portals largely formal English/Hindi me the. Regional language bolne wale karigar ya MSME owners easily interact nahi kar paate the.

1.3 The Solution (BIS Saathi):

Humne banaya BIS Saathi — ek authoritative, bilingual (22 Scheduled Indian Languages + English), multimodal regulatory cockpit jo: - Manufacturers ko 30 seconds me exact standard, testing protocols, aur printable document checklist bana kar deta hai. - Consumers ko 1-tap license validation, AI photo scanning for counterfeit detection, aur anonymous zero-PII grievance filing provide karta hai.

⚡ CHAPTER 7: DETERMINISTIC ALGORITHMS & REGEX ENGINES

7.1 Standard Mark Regex Engine

typescript
// ISI Scheme I license number format
const ISI_REGEX = /^CM\/L-\d{7,10}$/i;
// CRS Scheme II electronics registration format
const CRS_REGEX = /^R-\d{8}$/i;
- Why this regex? - Pehle BIS 7-digit numbers issue karta tha (e.g. CM/L-7654321). - Ab naye licenses me 10-digit numbers aate hain (e.g. CM/L-8400129384). - CRS electronics me R- ke baad exact 8 digits hote hain (e.g. R-41012345). - Humara regex dono ko seamlessly validate karta hai bina false negative ke.

7.2 Unicode-Safe Indic Language Detection

const isMarathi = /(साठी|कोणता|कोणती|कोणते|आहे|नाही|कसा|कसे|काय|करावे|सांगा|तपासा|खरेदी|बद्दल|मिळवायचे|लागतील|आहेत|दागिने|सोन्याचे|कारखान्यासाठी|प्रमाणपत्र|नियम|मराठी|माहिती|द्या|लागू|कशासाठी|हवे|पाहिजे|करायचे)/i.test(rawQuery);

📂 GROUP 1: TECHNICAL & SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

Q1: "Aapne Next.js 16 App Router kyun choose kiya standard React ke upar?"

Answer: "Next.js 16 App Router provides Server-Side Rendering (SSR) with Turbopack, jo humein zero Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS), fast First Contentful Paint (FCP < 600ms), aur Edge Route Handlers provide karta hai. Isse static catalogs edge CDN se serve hote hain aur server overhead minimum rehta hai."

Q2: "LLM hallucinations ko rokne ke liye aapka specific architecture kya hai?"

Answer: "Humara architecture 3-tiered defense use karta hai: (1) Deterministic regex aur database lookup critical license verification ke liye, (2) Gemini 3.6 Flash ka low temperature setting (0.2) with strict system prompt boundaries, aur (3) lib/fallbacks.ts jisme 20+ core standards ke verified statutory tables store hain."

Q3: "Web Speech API TTS me regional languages bina external paid API ke kaise chal rahi hain?"

Answer: "Hum browser ke native window.speechSynthesis API ka use kar rahe hain. Humne ek custom Unicode script analyzer likha hai jo response text ke character set (Devanagari, Tamil, Bengali, Telugu) ko identify karke browser ke available female Indian regional voice profile ko bind kar deta hai with 0 API cost."

Q4: "Aapka Rate Limiting algorithm memory leak kaise prevent karta hai?"

Answer: "Hum in-memory Map use karte hain jisme har IP ke saath ek resetAt timestamp store hota hai. Har 60 seconds ke baad window automatically reset hoti hai aur stale entries overwrite ho jaati hain."

Q5: "Agar government isse NIC Cloud / MeghRaj par deploy kare, toh kya changes lagenge?"

Answer: "Zero frontend changes. Backend me Next.js ko Docker container banakar NIC MeghRaj Kubernetes cluster par deploy kiya ja sakta hai, aur lib/gemini.ts me API endpoint ko on-premise hosted open-source model (jaise Llama-3-70B-Instruct) se point kiya ja sakta hai."

---

📂 GROUP 2: BIS DOMAIN & REGULATORY KNOWLEDGE

Q6: "Scheme I (ISI Mark) aur Scheme II (CRS) me exact process difference kya hai?"

Answer: "Scheme I (ISI Mark) safety critical products (helmets, cement, steel, pressure cookers) ke liye hai jisme physical factory inspection aur in-house lab (SIT) verification compulsory hoti hai before license grant. Scheme II (CRS) electronics aur IT goods ke liye hai jo Self-Declaration of Conformity (SDoC) aur NABL accredited lab test report ke basis par bina factory audit ke register hota hai."

Q7: "BIS Act 2016 ka Section 29 kya kehta hai?"

Answer: "Section 29 unauthorized standard mark usage, fake ISI marking, ya bina license mandatory QCO products bechne par penal provisions prescribe karta hai — jisme up to 2 years imprisonment aur minimum ₹5 lakh tak ka fine ya product value ka 10x penalty shamil hai."

Q8: "Gold Hallmarking me HUID ka kya role hai?"

Answer: "Under IS 1417:2016, HUID ek 6-digit alphanumeric unique laser identification code hai jo Assaying & Hallmarking Centre (AHC) par etch hota hai. Isse har jewellery piece central BIS database se link ho jaata hai, ensuring 100% purity traceability."

Q9: "FMCS scheme me AIR ka kya function hota hai?"

Answer: "Foreign Manufacturers Certification Scheme (FMCS) me overseas manufacturer ko India me resident ek Authorized Indian Representative (AIR) nominate karna hota hai jo BIS Act compliance, fee deposition, aur statutory inspection logistics ke liye legally responsible hota hai."

Q10: "Scheme of Inspection and Testing (SIT) kya hota hai?"

Answer: "SIT ek detailed testing schedule hai jo BIS har product ke liye define karta hai. Manufacturer ko apne factory ke in-house laboratory me un specific testing equipments ko install aur NABL-traceable calibrate karna mandatory hota hai."

---

📂 GROUP 3: PRIVACY, DPDP ACT & ETHICS
