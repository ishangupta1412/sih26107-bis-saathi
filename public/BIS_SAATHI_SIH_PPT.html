❖ Proposed Solution (Describe your Idea/Solution/Prototype)
  • Detailed explanation of the proposed solution:
    • BIS Saathi by Team Strophoid is an intelligent bilingual regulatory cockpit integrating Gemini 3.6 Flash multimodal AI with Supabase pgvector embeddings and deterministic license verification engines to streamline compliance for MSMEs and protect Indian citizens from counterfeit goods.
  • How it addresses the problem:
    • For Manufacturers / MSMEs: Resolves ambiguity across 350+ mandatory Quality Control Orders (QCOs); generates pre-audit statutory document checklists (Scheme I ISI, Scheme II CRS, FMCS) in 30 seconds instead of weeks.
    • For Consumers: Provides 1-tap verification of ISI CM/L- and CRS R- numbers + multimodal AI photo packaging scanning to spot counterfeit markings in local markets.
  • Innovation and uniqueness of the solution:
    • Deterministic + Generative Duality: Zero-hallucination regex checking for safety licenses + generative AI for regional language guidance.
    • Zero-PII Anonymous Whistleblower Mode: Server-side contact data purge under DPDP Act 2023 for reporting counterfeit cartels under Section 29 of the BIS Act, 2016.
    • Indian Web Accessibility Compliance: Engineered to IS 17802:2021 & GIGW 3.0 with native voice TTS in 22 Indian regional languages.
